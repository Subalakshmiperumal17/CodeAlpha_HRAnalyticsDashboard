# 👥 HR Workforce & Attrition Dashboard
An interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing workforce turnover, satisfaction, performance, and recruitment outlook using **1,470 employee records**.
## 🎯 Objective
To optimize HR processes and workforce management by:
- Tracking attrition rates across departments, age groups, tenure, overtime, and marital status  
- Analyzing employee satisfaction and performance  
- Forecasting hiring needs with predictive analytics  
## 📊 Dashboard Pages
1. **Workforce Analysis** → Headcount, department mix, attrition by department and age band  
2. **Employee Satisfaction & Performance** → Job/environment/work-life satisfaction, top performer rate, attrition by satisfaction level  
3. **Workforce Planning & Attrition Risk** → Attrition by overtime, job level, tenure band, marital status  
4. **Employee Demographics** → Gender, marital status, education field and level breakdowns  
5. **Recruitment & Hiring Forecast** → Proxy recruitment metrics, Key Influencers analysis, What-If hiring forecast  
## 🔍 Key Findings
- Overall attrition rate: **16.1%** (237 of 1,470 employees)  
- Overtime staff attrition: **30.5%** (vs 10.4% for non-overtime)  
- 43% of leavers had ≤2 years tenure → early-career retention is critical  
- Sales department has highest attrition (20.6%), followed by HR (19.0%)  
- Single employees leave at double the rate of married employees (25.5% vs 12.5%)  
- Projected hiring need: **311 new hires** (237 replacements + 74 growth at 5% expansion)  
## ⚙️ Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**  
- Dataset: *HR-Employee-Attrition.csv*  
- Predictive analytics: Key Influencers visual + What-If parameter
